# Code of conduct

apply follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be kind, welcome newcomers, and assume good faith.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- People here write in many languages and at many levels of English. Write plainly, and be patient with others.
- Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated, here or in the project's other spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@sanjiblamichhane](https://github.com/sanjiblamichhane), by
email to the address on that profile.
Every report is reviewed, and kept confidential. The maintainers will act as the Covenant's enforcement
guidelines describe: from a private word to a permanent ban.
