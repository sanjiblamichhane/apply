---
title: Saved answers
---

Answers to the questions forms keep asking (notice period, right to work, links), kept so you type them once. The kit and the extension use them (see [The kit](../kit/)).

- **Equal-opportunity questions are sensitive**: gender, ethnicity, veteran status, disability. They start as **Decline to answer**, and are never sent to a model.
- Add a question of your own with its exact words, so a form asking it word for word finds your answer.
- Each pack has its own questions.
