---
title: Applications
---

Everything you're applying for, in its pipeline.

1. **Paste** a link or the text. apply reads it into its fields; nothing is guessed, and you can correct any of it (see [The details](../opportunity/)).
2. Have its **documents** written from your profile, and check them (see [Documents](../documents/)).
3. **Apply** with the kit, then say you've sent it (see [The kit](../kit/)).
4. **Move** it through its stages: drag a card on the board, or use **Move to…**, the keyboard's way.

**Board** and **List** show the same applications; the filter finds one by title or organisation. Each pack has its own stages (see [Packs](../packs/)).

**Delete** on an application removes it, with its documents and history; its posting stays. **Undo** brings it back for a few seconds after. On a wide screen, ✕ closes an application beside the board.
