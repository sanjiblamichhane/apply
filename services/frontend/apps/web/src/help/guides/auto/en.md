---
title: Auto
---

Auto does the slow part of applying ahead of time, and you send each application yourself. It's off until you turn on **Prepare strong matches** in a search, and **Pause Auto** stops all of it at once.

1. A match scores at least your minimum, you're under the day's limit, and nothing went to the same employer in the last 30 days.
2. It's tracked, and its documents are written from your profile and checked. Any claim warning stops it.
3. It waits in **Ready for you**. **Open the form** opens the board's application form, where the extension fills it from your profile. Check everything and press Submit yourself. **Review** opens it in apply, and **Stop** takes it off the list.
4. When your submission is recorded (by the extension, or **I've submitted it**), it shows as sent.

apply never sends an application for you. Job boards give applicants no approved way to apply automatically, and their terms forbid automated access, so pressing Submit is always yours. Anything it couldn't prepare goes to **Needs you**, with the reason. The limits are in **Settings → Auto**.
