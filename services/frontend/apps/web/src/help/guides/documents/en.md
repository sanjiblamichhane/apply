Each document is written by your model from your profile and the details, in the background; you can leave the page while it works.

- **Nothing invented.** Documents may say only what your profile says. Anything missing is written as **[CONFIRM: …]** for you to fill in.
- **The claim check** reads each new version and flags what your profile doesn't back up: an employer, a number, a skill. Fix the text, or choose **It's true** if it is. A warning you've decided stays decided in later versions.
- **Write again** makes a new version; the one in use stays until the new one is ready. Older versions are under **Versions**.
- **Edit** saves your own version, which is checked too. **Reviewed** marks the version you've read through.
- **Preview** shows the version in use as it will be downloaded, in your template. **Download PDF** prints that page to a tagged, accessible PDF. **Download web page (.html)** saves the page itself.

### Your templates

Every document is made in one of your two templates: one for resumes (and other documents in sections), one for letters. Each starts as the default. You change them in **Settings → Document templates**.

1. **Download template**, then change its HTML and CSS: fonts, colours, spacing, where the name and contact line go.
2. Keep the placeholders apply fills in: `{{name}}`, `{{contact}}`, `{{content}}` (the document itself) and `{{title}}`. Also available: `{{date}}` (a letter's date), `{{description}}` and `{{lang}}`.
3. **Upload a template.** apply checks it first and says what to fix. It must be safe: no scripts, nothing fetched from elsewhere, images inline. Filled with a sample, it must also pass WCAG 2.2 AA: text at 4.5:1 contrast, links told apart from text, a visible focus, one main area, headings in order, zoom allowed.
4. When it passes, look at the sample, then press **Use this template**. Your documents are made again in it.

**Use the default** goes back to the default. **Undo**, right after, brings yours back.
