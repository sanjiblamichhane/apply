---
title: The browser extension
---

The browser extension keeps your profile in a panel beside any page. **Copy** is what it shows first: your basics, experience, education, skills and saved answers, in tabs, with one search across them. Choose a profile from the list at the top, which has its own search. Click a value to copy it, then paste it into the form, or type in the search and press Enter to copy the first match. Names come in parts, addresses line by line, and dates also as 01/2021 and 2021-01, the way forms ask. Equal-opportunity answers stay hidden until you show them. The panel touches nothing on the page, so it works on every site. The arrow-out-of-a-box icon opens apply in a new tab. Under **Settings** (the gear) you can choose the panel's look (Match the system, Light or Dark) and have the clipboard cleared a minute after each copy. That clears only what apply copied, and your computer's clipboard history keeps its own copy.

**Fill**, on the forms of supported job boards (or a site you allow with **Use apply on this site**), fills in your profile, attaches your PDFs and puts in your saved answers. First it shows what it would put in each field, with nothing written yet: untick anything you'd rather fill yourself, then press **Fill the form**. Anything it isn't sure of is left blank and listed. A board's own dropdowns are listed with the answer to choose, and you choose it. **You** check the form and press its Submit; the extension never does. When the board says thank you, it records the submission, with Undo.

To connect it, make a **token** here and paste it into the extension's panel. The token is shown once. It can read your profiles, read and track applications, and record submissions, nothing else. **Revoke** it whenever you like.
