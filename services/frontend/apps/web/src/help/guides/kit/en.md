title: The kit
---

Everything to apply anywhere by hand, in order.

1. **Open the form** on the organisation's own site.
2. **Download** the PDFs of the documents in use. Open claim warnings are shown here too: deal with them first (see [Documents](../documents/)).
3. **Copy** your saved answers into the form, one by one (see [Saved answers](../answers/)).
4. Press the form's own Submit, then **I've submitted it** here. That records what went out and moves the application on. You can **undo** it for 10 minutes.

The browser extension can do steps 2 and 3 for you on supported job boards, and on sites you allow (see [The extension](../extension/)).
