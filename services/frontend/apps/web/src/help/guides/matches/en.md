---
title: Matches
---

What your searches found, best fit first.

1. A **source** lists something (see [Sources](../sources/)).
2. A **search** checks it against its rules: the words it must have, where, and what rules it out (see [Searches](../searches/)).
3. What passes is **scored by your model** against your profile, from 0 to 100, with the reasons. **Watch out** marks a dealbreaker, such as a posting that won't sponsor a visa you need.

On each match: **Track** puts it on your board as an application; **Save** keeps it for later; **Dismiss** hides it (and **Put back** undoes that). The same listing found twice, on two boards, is one match.

A score is your model's view, not a fact: read the reasons.
