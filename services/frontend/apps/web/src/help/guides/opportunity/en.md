---
title: The details
---

What apply read from the notice or posting: its title, who it's from, and the rest of its fields, with the description underneath.

- **Read from the page** or **Read by your model** says how it was read. When it couldn't be read, nothing was guessed: fill in what the notice says, or **Read it again**.
- **Edit the details** corrects anything. Documents are written from these details and your profile, so a wrong detail makes a wrong document.
- Images in pasted text are never loaded; they show as their description.
