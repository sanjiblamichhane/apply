---
title: Packs
---

A pack is one kind of thing to apply for: **Jobs**, **Tenders**, **Grants** or **Contracts**. Choose one with **Working on** at the top of the sidebar; this device remembers it.

The pages then show and make that pack's things: its applications and their stages, its profiles, matches, searches, sources and saved answers. **Today** covers every pack.

Each pack writes its own documents: a resume and cover letter; a capability statement and tender response; a letter of inquiry and project narrative; a proposal and statement of work. Whatever the pack, documents say only what the profile says (see [Profiles](../profiles/)).
