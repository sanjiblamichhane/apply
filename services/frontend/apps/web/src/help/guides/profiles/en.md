---
title: Profiles
---

A profile is who's applying, and the facts documents may use. **Every document is written from a profile and nothing else**, so put in what you'd stand behind: roles and what you did in them, past contracts, programmes, results with their numbers.

- Each pack has its own profiles: a person for jobs; an organisation for tenders; an organisation or a researcher for grants; a freelancer or firm for contracts (see [Packs](../packs/)).
- One profile per pack is the **default**; an application can use another.
- **How documents should sound** takes plain instructions, like "short sentences, no jargon".
- Lists are one item per line. In groups such as past contracts, **Add** another and **Remove** one you don't need.

### Starting from your resume

On **New profile**, **Choose a file** (or drop one on the box): a PDF, a .docx file or text, up to 5 MB. The profile is made at once, and your model fills in its fields from the document: roles, education, skills and the rest. Any pack works: a tender's profile reads a capability statement.

- Your model is told to use only what the document says, and anything that doesn't fit a field is left out rather than guessed. It can still miss or misread something, so **check every field**, then save.
- Reading takes a minute or two. If you'd rather type it yourself, **Stop and fill it in myself**: what you type is never overwritten.
- If your model couldn't read it, **Read it again** once it's running. A scan with no text in it can't be read: upload the PDF or .docx file the resume was made in.
- The file is kept with the profile: **Download** it from the profile's page. Deleting the profile deletes the file.

### A second profile

**Duplicate** copies a profile, everything in it, as a new one that isn't the default. Change what's different (a summary for other roles, the skills to lead with) and save; the original stays as it was.
