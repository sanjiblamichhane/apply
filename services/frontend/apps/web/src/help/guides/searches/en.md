---
title: Searches
---

A search says what's worth your time. Only what passes it is scored by your model, so a good search saves time and keeps scores relevant.

- **Words to look for**: one per line, in the title or the description. Turn on **Every word must be there** to need all of them.
- **Words that rule it out**: checked in the title, like "senior" or "intern".
- **Places**, and, where the pack has them, **Remote only** and **I need visa sponsorship**.
- **Sources**: none chosen means every source (see [Sources](../sources/)).
- **Score against**: which profile the model compares with.
- **Prepare strong matches** (jobs only) turns on Auto for this search: strong matches are tracked and their documents written, and you send each one (see [Auto](../auto/)).

Saving a search looks through what's already here at once.
