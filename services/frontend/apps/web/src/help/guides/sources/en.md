---
title: Sources
---

A source is a public listing apply checks on a schedule: a company's own job board, UK tenders from Contracts Finder, or US grants from Grants.gov. What it finds goes through your searches (see [Searches](../searches/)).

- To follow a company's job board, press **Add a source** and paste the board's address (or one of its jobs') into **Job board address**. apply recognises a supported board and fills in the rest.
- Each source shows its terms, and apply never checks more often than they allow.
- **Check now** fetches it straight away; **Pause** stops it without removing it.
- A listing that disappears from its source is marked closed.
- Removing a source keeps what it already found.

Contracts have no public source: paste each brief in **Applications** instead.
