title: Today
---

What's new since yesterday, in one place, across every pack.

- **New matches**: the best of what your searches found, with each fit score. **See all matches** opens them (see [Matches](../matches/)).
- **Follow-ups**: applications that have sat in one stage longer than that stage expects. Open one to move it on or note what happened.
- **Closing soon**: deadlines in the next seven days.
- **Auto**: how many applications are ready for you to send, how many are being prepared, and how many need you (see [Auto](../auto/)).

The briefing is made each morning at the hour you set in **Settings**, in your time zone. **Brief me now** makes it straight away.
